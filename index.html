FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will the AI receptionist sound like a robot?

No — modern voice AI sounds natural and conversational. We customize it with your business name, services, hours, and tone, and you approve how it sounds before it ever answers a real call.

Do I need to be "techy" for any of this?

Not at all. We handle 100% of the setup and maintenance. You keep running your business — the only difference is your phone always gets answered and reviews start showing up.

How fast will I see results?

The AI receptionist starts capturing missed calls on day one. Most businesses see new Google reviews within the first two weeks of turning on the review engine.

Is asking customers for reviews allowed?

Yes — politely asking your real customers for honest reviews is completely fine. We never post fake reviews or offer incentives, which is what gets businesses in trouble.

What if I want to cancel?

No long-term contracts. Plans are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime — though once the reviews and booked appointments start rolling in, most clients don't.
