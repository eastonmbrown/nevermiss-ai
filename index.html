FAQ

Questions, answered.

What happens during the AI Audit?

We spend 30-45 minutes learning how your business runs today, then hand you a clear written report on where AI could save you real time and money — no jargon, no pressure, no obligation to buy anything.

Do I need to be "techy" for any of this?

Not at all. We handle 100% of the setup and maintenance. You keep running your business — the only difference is the busywork starts disappearing and the reviews start showing up.

How fast will I see results?

Most businesses see new Google reviews within the first two weeks of turning on the review engine. With training, teams usually start saving time the same week they learn the workflows.

Is asking customers for reviews allowed?

Yes — politely asking your real customers for honest reviews is completely fine. We never post fake reviews or offer incentives, which is what gets businesses in trouble.

What if I want to cancel?

No long-term contracts. Monthly plans are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime — though once the reviews start rolling in, most clients don't.
