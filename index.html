FAQ

Questions, answered.

What happens during the AI Audit?

We spend 30 to 45 minutes learning how your business runs today, then hand you a clear written report on where AI could save you real time and money. No jargon, no pressure, no obligation to buy anything.

Do I need to be "techy" for any of this?

Not at all. We handle the setup and maintenance, and training is built around the work your team already does. The only difference you'll notice is the busywork shrinking and the reviews showing up.

How fast will I see results?

The review engine starts sending requests the day it goes live, so new Google reviews typically begin within the first couple of weeks. Training pays off the same week your team learns the workflows.

What does Speed-to-Lead actually do?

When someone fills out your form, calls and gets no answer, or messages you, they get a personal text and email back within about a minute, even at 9 PM on a Saturday. Then a short follow-up sequence keeps checking in until they book or reply. You get an alert on your phone for every new lead. The reason it works is simple: the first business to respond usually gets the job.

Is asking customers for reviews allowed?

Yes. Politely asking your real customers for honest reviews is completely fine. We never post fake reviews or offer incentives, which is what gets businesses in trouble.

Do you work outside Heber City?

Yes. In-person work covers the Wasatch Back and Wasatch Front, including Park City, Provo, and Salt Lake City. Everything can also be done online, wherever you are.

You're a new company. Why should I trust you?

Because nothing is hidden. Prices are fixed and published, the audit gives you a written report you keep whether or not you continue, plans are month-to-month, and you get the founder's direct phone number.

What if I want to cancel?

No long-term contracts. Monthly plans are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime.
